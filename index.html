Portfolio web design

Role
Web designer
Timeline

Tools

The problem

What was broken?

As a beginner, I had no portfolio and no projects to show yet, so I had no way to share my skills.

The solution

What did you do?

As a beginner, I designed my own portfolio in Figma. I made five pages, chose a purple, gold and black style, and linked them into a clickable prototype.

The outcome

What changed?

I now have a portfolio design with a clickable prototype, and I learned layout, color and prototyping.
